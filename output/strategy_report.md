## 1. Executive Summary  

- **Thermal Unit Strategy**: Run the 600 MW coal-fired unit at full capacity (600 MW) for all 24 hours, avoiding any start-up/shut-down cycles. Although three hours (02:00–04:00) have market prices below marginal cost (220–240 vs. 248 yuan/MWh), the total losses (≈32,400 yuan) are far outweighed by the start-up cost savings (500,000 yuan) and by the high margins in the remaining 21 hours.  
- **Renewable Integration**: All renewable output (1682.9 MWh total) is fully utilized and bid at zero price. It is the lowest-cost generation and must be taken.  
- **Contract Fulfillment**: The 500 MWh contract at 380 yuan/MWh is physically delivered from hour 04:00 (the hour with the lowest spot price, 220 yuan/MWh). This maximizes the value of the fixed‑price contract by allocating it to the cheapest period.  
- **Spot Bidding**: All generation (renewable + thermal) is bid at zero price. This guarantees acceptance in all hours, avoids complex multi‑bid strategies, and simply collects the day‑ahead clearing price.  
- **Expected Daily Profit**: ~3.74 million yuan (contract + spot revenue minus fuel cost).

## 5. Risk Scenario Analysis  

### Scenario A: Renewable Output 20% Below Forecast  
- Renewable total becomes 0.8 × 1682.9 = 1,346.3 MWh  
- Thermal unchanged (14,400 MWh)  
- Total generation = 15,746.3 MWh  
- Contract still deliverable from hour 04 (generation 609.8 × 0.8 + 600 = 607.84 > 500)  
- New renewable spot value: 0.8 × 903,061 = 722,449 yuan  
- Gross spot revenue = 6,330,000 + 722,449 = 7,052,449 yuan  
- Net spot revenue = 7,052,449 – 110,000 = 6,942,449 yuan  
- Total revenue = 190,000 + 6,942,449 = 7,132,449 yuan  
- Profit = 7,132,449 – 3,571,200 = **3,561,249 yuan**  
- **Change: –180,612 yuan (–4.8%)** — low impact due to high thermal base.

### Scenario B: Market Prices Drop 30%  
- All spot prices multiplied by 0.7  
- New sum of prices = 10,550 × 0.7 = 7,385  
- Thermal spot value = 600 × 7,385 = 4,431,000 yuan  
- Renewable spot value = 903,061 × 0.7 = 632,143 yuan  
- Gross spot revenue = 4,431,000 + 632,143 = 5,063,143 yuan  
- Contract hour price becomes 220 × 0.7 = 154 yuan/MWh  
- Net spot revenue = 5,063,143 – (500 × 154) = 5,063,143 – 77,000 = 4,986,143 yuan  
- Total revenue = 190,000 + 4,986,143 = 5,176,143 yuan  
- Profit = 5,176,143 – 3,571,200 = **1,604,943 yuan**  
- **Change: –2,136,918 yuan (–57.1%)** — high exposure to price risk.

### Risk Mitigation Recommendations  
- **Price risk is the dominant threat.** Consider hedging with financial swaps or reducing thermal output when price forecasts are weak.  
- **Renewable risk is manageable** given the small share (≈10% of energy). No additional hedging needed.  
- Maintain a **minimum of 500 MW thermal** during morning/evening peaks even if prices dip, to preserve ramp capability for the next high-price period.
